# Privacy Policy: GHOSTNET: Deep Dive

Last updated: 11 October 2026

GHOSTNET: Deep Dive ("the game") is a single-player game made by a student team.

## What the game collects

Nothing. The game:

- does not collect, store or share any personal information,
- does not use accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting,
- does not connect to the internet while you play,
- asks for no Android permissions.

## What is stored on your device

Your progress (save slots) and your settings (volume, screen options) are saved only on your own device, inside
the game's private storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
